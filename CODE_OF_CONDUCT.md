# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a harassment-free, inclusive community for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status,
nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, sexual identity and
orientation, or technical preference.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing concrete impact.
- Accept correction and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Keep private information and security reports private.
- Focus criticism on the work, never the person.

Harassment, threats, sexualized conduct, discriminatory language, doxxing,
deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, and retaliation are not allowed.
This applies in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `conduct@distilled.news`. That address is
not considered operational until the launch record contains an externally sent
test message and a reply from the monitored destination. Hosted registration
and public community promotion must remain closed until that test passes.

GitHub's private vulnerability-reporting form is for security vulnerabilities,
not ordinary conduct complaints. Maintainers will protect reporter privacy as
far as practical, assess context, and may warn, remove content, temporarily
restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant. Maintainers who have
a conflict must recuse themselves.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
