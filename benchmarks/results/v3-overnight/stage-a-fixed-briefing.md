Replit makes OpenAI its default AI partner, reduces reliance on Anthropic
Replit CEO Amjad Masad said at TechCrunch Disrupt on October 16 that OpenAI's ChatGPT is now the default model for Replit's AI coding platform, replacing Anthropic's Claude models due to cost, performance, and partnership factors. While Anthropic models remain available, most users are expected to use the default, likely reducing Replit's spend with Anthropic. Masad stated that enterprise contracts now make up about 15% of Replit's revenue, up from 2% last year, and expects more than half of revenue to come from enterprise and government customers in 2027. Some insiders dispute the extent of the shift away from Anthropic, and recent Replit materials still promote Anthropic models.

Anthropic commits 5% of shares to new public benefit fund
Anthropic announced today that it will allocate 5% of its shares to a 'public benefit fund' aimed at supporting projects for societal good, with the option to contribute an additional 2% for every 50% increase in company value. The fund, structured as a wholly-owned entity, may make large donations to nonprofits or direct distributions. Anthropic said it will provide more details on the fund's governance in the coming months.

Laplace Intelligence develops AI-driven red-teaming for robotics and autonomous vehicles
Laplace Intelligence, a startup founded by Howie Sun and Henry Liu, is offering automated red-teaming for robotics and physical AI, using high-fidelity simulations and AI agents to test for failures before real-world deployment. The company, spun out of University of Michigan research, is generating revenue from autonomous driving clients and is piloting smart city solutions with Nvidia and T-Mobile. Laplace aims to scale its technology and transition to recurring revenue.
