Trump suspends several Russia sanctions amid dispute with Ukraine
US President Donald Trump issued an executive order suspending several Russia sanctions for 60 days, two days before they were due to take effect. The sanctions, passed by Congress and signed into law in September, targeted Russian oil and gas exports, senior officials, financial institutions, and vessels used to evade embargoes. Secretary of State Marco Rubio said the pause is to analyse the global economic impact. The move drew criticism from US lawmakers and Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky, who said the sanctions could have pressured Russia to end the war. Russia welcomed the suspension. The decision follows a US deal to import Russian diesel and comes amid strained US-Ukraine relations and recent Russian attacks on Ukrainian energy infrastructure.

Kromberg & Schubert plant in Lutsk halts after Russian drone attack
The Kromberg & Schubert electrical wiring plant in Lutsk, Ukraine, stopped operations after being hit by Russian drones on October 16, the company announced. The facility, which employs 5,500 workers and supplies major automakers, will remain closed until further notice while safety is assessed. Lutsk, far from the front lines, has seen repeated Russian strikes, including a drone attack on October 14 that killed six people.
