### [new, importance 2] Anthropic gives METR employee-like access for independent risk assessment
Anthropic said it has started giving a team from METR, a nonprofit that evaluates frontier AI models, employee-like access. It has onboarded six METR employees with company hardware, office space and access to internal tools and systems roughly on par with what its own risk assessment teams have. Anthropic employees are encouraged to share information with them as they would with a fellow employee, outside a small number of clearly defined categories such as customer data, attorney-client privileged information and model architecture information. METR will publish its analysis; Anthropic cannot veto publication but can require redaction of information in predefined categories such as confidential IP. Anthropic is also working with SecureBio and Accenture as evaluators and is in early discussions with several other organizations, including Hugging Face.
