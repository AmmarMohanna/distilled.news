# Publication-date admission and resolved-evidence recovery

Publication time owns the normal reporting window; evidence must also be observed and, for cutoff editions, acquired before cutoff. Original publication dates remain unchanged. Late discovery/acquisition, changed revisions, explicit verification retries and genuinely unresolved missed windows remain eligible. No monetary or editorial quotas are introduced.

The confirmed defect is that missed-window recovery excludes only published citations. It reoffers unchanged revisions explicitly skipped, omitted or dropped as redundant in earlier completed editorial results. Read the existing candidate-indexed decisions as both retry and resolution state, scoped through the preceding reporting boundary. A selected verified item whose publication has not completed is unresolved, not an editorial omission. Missing/unaccounted decisions remain unresolved; do not invent relevance judgments.

New snapshots record the admission policy and a reason per source (publication window, late discovery, source revision, explicit verification retry or unresolved missed window). Frozen snapshots, paid identities, reservations, receipts and legacy result documents are never rewritten. The same backend policy applies to every feed. Existing source authorization, privacy and final publication validation remain intact.

Use the existing carry index with a consistent generation across pages. Retain the existing fallback for schemas without the index/backfill. Avoid downloading unrelated historical result bodies on indexed deployments.

Validation includes skipped/omitted/redundant receipts, verification retries, unaccounted sources, verified-but-unpublished items, changed revisions, late acquisition, date boundaries, future timestamps, cross-feed isolation, historical recovery, immutable paid replay, and a read-only real-staging admission comparison. Actual removed candidates must have a prior explicit resolution; real news text and financial records stay retained.

Implementation rulings:
- Known reader communication and verified-but-undelivered selection are distinct. Pending verified selections remain recoverable until a published edition records their use.
- Mechanical duplicate grouping and absent consolidation explanations are unresolved, not editorial declines. Old safety-cap omissions remain explicit overflow recovery.
- A later/future reporting result cannot suppress an earlier chronological input. Invalid legacy reporting dates do not resolve earlier evidence.
- Read indexed decision identities first, then compact reference/reason metadata once per relevant result across all pages. Recheck generation after metadata reads. The old carry-only API retains positive-only seeking and its original truthiness/order semantics.
- A read-only real comparison on the retained 15:30 Beirut window excluded 219 of 958 candidates. Every exclusion had an earlier explicit resolution. The other 739 remained eligible; most were unresolved missed-window evidence, not a fresh-news claim. Original publication dates and the original frozen fingerprint were unchanged.
