# Distilled.news — Agent Performance and Evaluation Evidence

**Evidence cutoff:** November 23, 2026. **Repository reference:** `e5f4ffb2` on `main`. This index extends the [October 28 evaluation report](../evaluation/briefing-quality/reports/FINAL_REPORT.md) with completed November experiments, production observations and negative results.

Distilled.news has demonstrated evidence-linked multilingual publication, a short headline layer with details available on demand, and specific improvements in source handling and recovery. The team also tested where the pipeline and its judges fail. The available pairs against Single LLM are mixed and frequently fail the common output contract. The evidence supports specific comparisons and engineering results. Population-wide factual accuracy, reader benefit and overall superiority remain unmeasured.

## What was evaluated

| Contribution | Unit and method | Success measures and boundaries |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Product performance** | A briefing window with its historical feed interests, sources available by cutoff, and actual prior editions. Inspect the published headline and expansion together, with layer-specific counts for reading effort. For controlled pairs, freeze comparison inputs and a source-first checklist before opening the drafts. | Report concision, relevance, factual support, citation correctness and novelty separately. Distinguish essential information and qualifications from optional detail. A shorter briefing can lose meaning. Quiet can be appropriate when the evidence supports it. |
| **Evaluator reliability** | Positive and deliberately corrupted versions of the same case, checked against source passages and reader history. Record semantic judgment, evidence binding, response structure and operational failures separately. | A resolved citation establishes provenance. Factual support requires evidence for the full assertion, including attribution, conditions and relationships. Contradicted, insufficiently supported and uncertain judgments remain distinct. Judge diagnostic scores measure the evaluator's behavior on controls. |
| **System engineering** | Real publications and provider receipts, plus separately labeled offline regression, load and fault fixtures. | Track admission, publication, recovery, latency, calls, tokens, settled cost and unresolved exposure. Local fixture throughput and observed production latency describe different workloads. |

The baseline ladder answers different questions. **All-headlines** measures unfiltered reading effort. **Keyword filtering** tests lexical personalization with up to five items, without translation or semantic novelty. **Single LLM** uses one GPT-4.1 writing call without the pipeline's verification and repair. The fresh comparison shared the writing model, frozen sources, feed and language, prior briefing, cutoff, citation map and 75-word maximum. Earlier archive pairs had looser budgets and evolving per-arm histories. Within-pipeline comparisons held a production or V4 baseline against one proposed change. ## Consolidated evidence

**Reading the table.** *Mechanical* means deterministic validation or an operational receipt. *Source inspection* means a scoped comparison with retained text, with uncertainty where evidence is incomplete. *Model judgment* means a saved automated label. A live model output is an observation of what an arm wrote. None of these labels constitutes independent human validation.

The six paid Arabic pairs included three news windows, two correction/history controls and a quiet-selection diagnostic.
