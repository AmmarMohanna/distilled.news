## Assessment, kept separate

1. **Operational delivery:** Accepted nonempty outputs, justified quiet outcomes, source-specific holds, contract failures and repair/recheck behavior. A structural hold is not factual-error detection.
2. **Factual meaning:** Approval conditions, redaction versus veto rights, quantities, time, actor attribution and source-specific support. Accept valid concise paraphrases and headline-only controls; do not demand optional background. Inspect final accepted text after repairs.
3. **Useful distillation:** Main development and important new details retained, distinct developments separated, complementary same-event evidence combined, meaningful updates distinguished from previous coverage. Count repetition, filler and headline/body duplication separately. Do not reward word count or more citations by themselves.
4. **Efficiency:** Actual settled editor + verifier + repair cost, tokens, cache usage, calls and measured pipeline/provider times per paired case. Include failures and unsuccessful expenditure; do not compare failed early exits as faster successful delivery. The target is no more than 10% added cost/latency without material quality regressions.

All semantic findings require original source passages and specific offending/omitted text. Assessments remain AI-assisted engineering judgments, not human gold or statistical factual-error rates. Ambiguous findings stay unresolved. No paid external judge is included.
