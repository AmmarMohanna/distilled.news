Z completed source processing and editorial planning, returned a Haiku draft, ran the existing automatic repair and then factual verification. It did **not** persist an edition. The job ended `INVALID_REQUEST` after its existing repair allowance; no operator override or second trial was used.

Independent AI-assisted source review also rejected the briefing's editorial quality. The insider's statement that the Anthropic partnership remained in place was deferred as less critical than the CEO's account and was absent from reader prose. That is necessary counter-evidence, not optional background. Continued access to Anthropic and the later default-provider clarification **were** present; they must not be reported as missing. Fragmentation put the counterclaim in a separate candidate, so the selected-candidate disagreement guard did not require it. Laplace was spread across core product, simulation mechanics and a standalone CEO moat statement, while automatic corner-case agents—the source's distinctive product detail—were deferred. Anthropic's 5%, optional 2% per 50% valuation increase, wholly-owned structure and pending governance were retained. Replit revenue was explicitly deferred; not every omitted company metric needs to appear, but omitting the opposing partnership account changes the selected development's meaning.

These failures are not invalid-reference or JSON failures. The identity challenge prevented unsupported cross-company continuity; it did not resolve intra-article fragmentation or guarantee fair editorial coverage. The verifier caught the date error but did not independently flag every issue identified in the separate source review.
