# AgentOps incident runbooks

For the proposed fifteen-minute Worker collector, keep alerts paused until cadence-aware thresholds and missingness are validated. Do not apply shorter absence thresholds unchanged or infer continuous health from latest-window gauges. See [activation approval](ACTIVATION_APPROVAL.md) for the currently blocked deployment and rollback procedure. No legacy-log export is authorized.

All thresholds are provisional review policies. Preserve one incident per service/alertname; group for 1 minute, update at most every 10 minutes, repeat after 12 hours while unresolved. Do not page repeatedly for every request or add feed/operation IDs to labels. No live notification policy has been changed.

## Publication
Trigger: at least three scoped failed requests for 10 minutes, or known recorded publication p95 delay above 600000 ms for 10 minutes. Review sample count and cohort first. These are triage settings without baseline evidence, not an SLA. Trace exact original window, request reason, verification, publication status and coverage. Distinguish quiet/deferred/blocked and persisted-but-unconfirmed. Compare scheduledFor and publishedAt, never rewrite cutoff. Confirm independent public-reader evidence before calling delivery recovered. Do not regenerate paid work with a new identity.

## Backlog
Trigger: more than 25 retained pending/running acquisition/reassessment jobs for 15 minutes. Confirm cohort completeness and missing source states. Inspect last poll attempts, resource waits, quota/config failures, queue outcomes and existing leases. Legacy job rows lack enqueue timestamps; the new known-subset proxy and missingness counts must be inspected together. A separate timestamp-aware prospective observer is needed for age or backlog growth alerts. Do not reset leases or delete jobs. Stop a read-only load campaign if errors/backlog rise; continue existing authorized recovery only. Resolve after the original cohort clears and a declared stability interval is observed, not after arrivals stop.

## Provider
Trigger: at least three retained SEMANTIC_PROVIDER_FAILURE/HTTP failures for 10 minutes or observed invocation error fraction above 5% for 10 minutes. These are different populations. Inspect Cloudflare provider requests and D1 binding spans to attribute failures; this generic invocation alert does not establish a database-specific failure rate. Review provider receipts, HTTP codes and account resource waits, preserving paid identities and quarantine. Do not resubmit unknown paid outcomes. Confirm a controlled successful attempt and stable error fraction before resolution. Historical failure gauges do not decrease on recovery: close retained-cohort alerts by advancing the explicitly documented cohort, not deleting records.

## Finance
Trigger: any unsettled operation persisting for 15 minutes or confirmed partial retained cost increase above USD 5/hour on an unchanged complete cohort. Severity critical for unsettled exposure; a short normal in-flight operation should not fire. Confirm immutable reservation/settlement identities, unknown outcomes, recovery receipts and original authorized policy. Provider-reported, settled, reserved and held values have different meanings; do not add them as spend. Missing charge information prevents a full actual-cost conclusion. Never infer spending authority from reservations. Authorized-limit utilization alert is not implemented because the trace lacks the relevant account limit snapshot. Existing authority/ledger/reconciliation remains unchanged. Escalate to the account operator; no new spending or financial mutation is authorized by an alert.

## Observer
Trigger: no scheduled agentops event for 10 minutes, held for 2 further minutes, with unsampled logs and a one-minute cron. Check the actual cron schedule before enabling. Inspect destination delivery errors, service_name/envelope mapping, AGENTOPS_ENABLED, sampling and Grafana ingestion health. Distinguish no work, missing invocation, broken observer and unavailable source data. Check external snapshot collector freshness separately; the supplied cron rule cannot prove a snapshot is fresh. Remove stale textfiles instead of silently retaining healthy-looking old gauges. An absent metric is not zero. Test one approved isolated event, its private trace and ingestion timestamp to recover.

## Enqueue-age and V3 timing extension
New canonical jobs record a real wall-clock enqueuedAt precommit proxy in the existing immutable job write; no new write or identity is introduced. The existing grouped sources query now returns oldest timestamp, observed/missing timestamp coverage, attempts and recorded resource waits. Retained legacy jobs are not backfilled. Oldest observed pending age is a known-subset lower bound, never proof that older unmeasured work does not exist. The supplemental paused age alert uses a provisional 30-minute threshold held 15 minutes.

V3 already persists total/stage timings in v3_window_results. Overview now reads that exact request-bound result by indexed ID and exports only fixed timing stages (edit, verify, repair, consolidate) and status. No prompt or reader text is exported. Stage timing includes existing engine/reuse behavior and uses the engine's existing wall clock; it is not a claim of fresh provider execution. Recorded publication proxies use publication_status.publishedAt, edition.publishedAt or edition.createdAt in that order with explicit source provenance.

Snapshot observer alerts use distilled_observer_up and minimum private trace observedAt. A value of zero or absence held two minutes requires diagnosis. Input age above five minutes held two minutes is stale; never relabel old files with a new timestamp. The exporter generation timestamp is a separate fact.

## Export privacy and collector admission

Before enabling a destination, review [STAGING_ACTIVATION.md](STAGING_ACTIVATION.md). Metadata-only AgentOps events do not make every existing console/error/span field safe. Inspect a controlled OTLP sample; remove raw errors, source text, account metadata and sensitive URL/SQL attributes before broad export. Preserve native trace correlation without turning record IDs into labels. If the private snapshot collector or its administrator authentication expires, expose observer failure/staleness rather than replaying old healthy metrics. Dashboard availability alone does not prove collector health.
