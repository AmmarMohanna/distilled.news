# Acceptable Use Policy

Effective: 29 July 2026

This policy defines prohibited use of the hosted Distilled.news service.

## Prohibited uses

Do not use Distilled.news to:

- break the law, violate sanctions, or facilitate fraud or deception;
- threaten, harass, stalk, dox, exploit, or incite violence against a person;
- publish private, intimate, or sensitive personal data without a lawful basis;
- infringe copyright, database, trademark, privacy, publicity, or contractual rights;
- distribute malware, phishing, spam, credential theft, or harmful code;
- evade authentication, rate, budget, provider, retention, or suspension controls;
- probe, disrupt, overload, scrape, or automate the service without permission;
- impersonate another person or misrepresent a synthetic feed as a real person;
- use source integrations contrary to publisher or provider terms;
- create high-stakes automated decisions about people using generated summaries;
- interfere with security investigations or conceal the origin of abusive activity.

## Security research and enforcement

Security research that follows [the security policy](../../SECURITY.md) is
permitted. Good-faith mistakes will be handled proportionately, but urgent harm
may require immediate restriction.

Report abuse to `abuse@distilled.news` with the public URL, reason, supporting
evidence, and a safe contact address. The operator may preserve relevant
records, limit visibility, suspend processing, remove content, or disable an
account while investigating.

## Changes

Material changes will update the effective date. Existing hosted accounts must
accept the current policy before making account, feed, source, or star changes;
logout and account deletion remain available.
