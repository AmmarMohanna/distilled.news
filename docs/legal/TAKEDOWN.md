# Content and takedown process

Distilled.news publishes generated summaries and links based on public sources.
It does not control source pages. Requests about the original publication
should usually also be sent to the source publisher.

Send a request to `legal@distilled.news` with:

- the exact Distilled.news URL and the specific material at issue;
- the legal or factual basis for the request;
- the requester's name, role, and reliable contact information;
- evidence of authority when acting for another person or rights holder;
- the requested action and any urgency or safety concern;
- a good-faith statement that the information is accurate.

Copyright notices should identify the protected work, the allegedly infringing
material, the rights holder, and the requester's authority. Knowingly false
claims may create liability.

The operator may acknowledge receipt, preserve evidence, restrict or remove the
material, ask for clarification, notify the affected user, or reject an
unsupported request. Counter-notices and appeals should reference the original
case and explain why the action should be reversed. Emergency threats should be
reported to the appropriate local authorities as well as the service.

Removal from Distilled.news cannot remove source publications, search-engine
caches, archives, screenshots, syndication, or copies controlled by others.
