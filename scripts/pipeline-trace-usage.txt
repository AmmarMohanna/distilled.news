Distilled.news operator Pipeline Trace

Status: implemented on v1/pipeline-trace. Deployment requires separate approval.
No source fetch, paid call, enrollment, retry, ledger projection, or financial
write occurs when reading a trace. No migrations or new history tables.

1. Create a test Feed through the ordinary signed-in Feed editor after deployment.
Choose interests, permitted sources, visibility and schedule. Let normal polling
and the scheduler run; there is no manual Brief now action. This document does
not authorize creating test Feeds or running paid operations.

2. Find its ID while signed in as an existing platform administrator:
GET /api/admin/briefings
Use briefing.id, not its slug. Non-admin account sessions cannot access traces,
including their own private Feeds. Platform administrators have existing
platform-wide diagnostic authority; every query is still scoped to the requested
Feed, and foreign source/evidence/edition/candidate references are rejected.

3. Read a window (UTC boundaries; both required when either supplied):
GET /api/admin/briefings/<feed-id>/pipeline-trace?view=overview&windowStart=2026-10-13T00%3A00%3A00Z&windowEnd=2026-10-13T02%3A00%3A00Z
Use view=sources for configuration, actual providers, receipts, coverage and jobs.
Use view=articles for discovery, acquisition, source timing, content completeness,
claim-to-Event lineage, semantic-operation references, editions and ledger IDs.
Use view=candidates for the full parent shortlist, including overflow, approved
facts, relevant reader history, relevance provenance, final recorded treatment,
accepted model judgment, writing/verification and communication evidence.
Use view=operations for models, failures, repairs, token usage, latency, reported
cost and held exposure. Window filtering includes only directly window/selection-
bound operations; shared article semantics must be inspected by evidence ID.
Without a window, editorialContextWindow identifies the latest retained snapshot.

4. Missing article (do not combine an old-article lookup with a new window):
GET .../pipeline-trace?view=articles&sourceId=<owned-source>&url=<URL-encoded-article-url>
Discovery batches are included even without accepted intake. Exact recorded URLs
are matched after URL parsing/fragment removal; arbitrary URLs are never fetched.
No match means NOT_OBSERVABLE, not proof that the publisher had no article or
that an editor rejected it. Redirect/alias identities absent from the records
cannot be inferred. Follow an acquired evidence ID with:
GET .../pipeline-trace?view=operations&evidenceRevisionId=<revision-id>

5. Candidate omitted, suppressed, deferred or not considered:
GET .../pipeline-trace?view=candidates&targetId=<Event-or-Storyline-id-or-version>&windowStart=<UTC-start>&windowEnd=<UTC-end>
The same view accepts candidateId, sourceId or evidenceRevisionId. A final
recordedDecision is separate from an acceptedComparativeJudgment. decision is
null when no accepted real-model judgment is established. Never interpret a
technical DEFER/overflow as irrelevance. Inspect modelOperationIds,
globalContextOperationIds, planId, flags and pendingDelivery. Exact mandatory-fact
communication requires ledger_fact_bindings; one ledger sentence is insufficient.

6. Failed publication and original retained records:
GET .../pipeline-trace?view=records&kind=briefing_requests&windowStart=<UTC-start>&windowEnd=<UTC-end>
GET .../pipeline-trace?view=records&kind=synthesis_jobs&recordId=<request-id>
GET .../pipeline-trace?view=records&kind=editorial_proposals&windowStart=<UTC-start>&windowEnd=<UTC-end>
GET .../pipeline-trace?view=records&kind=drafts&recordId=<draft-id>
GET .../pipeline-trace?view=records&kind=verification_results&recordId=<verification-id>
GET .../pipeline-trace?view=records&kind=verification_feedback&candidateId=<candidate-id>
GET .../pipeline-trace?view=records&kind=publication_status&recordId=<edition-id>
GET .../pipeline-trace?view=records&kind=ledger_entries&candidateId=<candidate-id>
GET .../pipeline-trace?view=records&kind=correction_obligations
GET .../pipeline-trace?view=records&kind=correction_resolutions
GET .../pipeline-trace?view=records&kind=delivery_jobs&recordId=<edition-id>
Other allowed kinds include semantic_construction_work/progress,
editorial_processing_work/progress/waits, publication_work,
publication_reconciliations, rematch_requests/attempts, event_semantic_states,
events/versions, storylines/versions, entities, propositions and state_slots.
Raw model prompts/provider responses, token/lease ownership and credentials are
withheld. Proposals retain rejection, attempt, usage and original record ID;
rawProposal is deliberately not echoed. Inspect exact retained raw responses only
through an independently authorized database investigation if necessary.

7. Costs:
GET .../pipeline-trace?view=operations&recordId=<operation-id>
Reported costs use account settlements when available, otherwise persisted usage.
Held/UNKNOWN exposure is never labeled confirmed spending. A late known settlement
does not make an UNKNOWN canonical result usable. Feed accounting totals cover
mapped operations, not all platform spending; unmappedIntentCount is explicit.
windowSpending is a directly bound subtotal, not the total cost of shared semantics.
Legacy reuse without a durable alias receipt remains NOT_OBSERVABLE.

8. CLI (run from repository root):
Set DISTILLED_TRACE_COOKIE through a secure process environment containing an
existing operator session. Never include it in command arguments or a file.
The CLI refuses non-HTTPS origins and redirects and does not print credentials.
node scripts/pipeline-trace.mjs --origin https://distilled-news-staging.distillednews-platform.workers.dev --feed <feed-id> --view candidates --window-start <UTC-start> --window-end <UTC-end> --all
node scripts/pipeline-trace.mjs --feed <feed-id> --view articles --url <article-url> --all
node scripts/pipeline-trace.mjs --feed <feed-id> --view operations --evidence <revision-id> --all
node scripts/pipeline-trace.mjs --input <sanitized-local-trace.json>
Add --json for structured output. pageSize (1..100) is only a transport page size;
--all follows all cursors. Counts and cursor scope changes abort pagination.
Do not publish private diagnostic exports or commit retained evidence.
Unsupported filters return INVALID_REQUEST rather than being silently ignored.

Limitations:
- Live SELECTs are not one transaction snapshot; immutable identities are stable,
  but requests/jobs may progress between pages. Rerun on concurrent changes.
- Source metadata is last known, not proof every enabled source was polled in a
  given window. Poll-job lookup includes jobs without an executed fetch run. Legacy poll-job
  tables lack Feed-scope indexes: this uses the existing health query pattern
  on queue metadata, not raw article bodies. A source-team scope index is
  recommended before large-platform diagnostic polling.
- Missing instrumentation, frontend/device receipt and historical reuse receipts
  remain NOT_OBSERVABLE. Push-service acceptance is not device delivery.
- Published means an immutable edition exists; currentlyPublished means its
  publication status remains PUBLISHED. Neither proves frontend rendering or
  public access to a private Feed. Withdrawal preserves historical communication.
- Semantic/window counts that cannot be faithfully attributed remain null with
  reasons. Different entities (observations, revisions, claims, Events, stories)
  are never presented as interchangeable article counts.

Staging live test plan (prepared; not executed by this task):
Use Lebanon News, AI Developments and broad international news with overlapping
approved sources, after separate deployment/live-test authorization. Inventory
actual source permissions/providers and compare publisher article URLs with raw
batch discovery and accepted evidence for at least two normal closed windows.
For each article verify extraction coverage/timing, claim lineage, distinct versus
shared Events, Storyline continuity and unresolved operations. For every candidate
compare Feed interests and reader history with accepted global model judgment,
including overflow and technical waits. Compare selected mandatory facts with
writer packet inputs, verifier results, final edition and exact ledger bindings.
Check omissions independently, not solely from planner rationales. Audit source
fidelity, corrections, withdrawal, novelty, duplicate delivery and public/private
reader access. Report actual account usage separately from held exposure and shared
cost attribution. The shared $50/7-day staging authorization is context, not
permission to activate/change a grant, budgets, concurrency or provider credentials.

Request status is projected at summary.publication.briefingRequest. This is a
whitelist of durable status/coverage fields; raw provider request bodies remain
redacted. The readable CLI also accepts older saved reports with publication.request.
