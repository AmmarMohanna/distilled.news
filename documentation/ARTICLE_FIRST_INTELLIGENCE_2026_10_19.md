## Narrow implementation

`construction-transport.ts` now instructs the same construction call to:

1. Read the complete article and identify its principal occurrence before assigning supporting facts.
2. Keep terms, uses, motivations, quotation, history and future details with the related occurrence unless another distinct action/update/stage warrants a separate group. No one-article/one-event or group-count rule.
3. Compare occurrences against offered canonical E/L identities: SAME_EVENT for another account/detail/correction of that occurrence, NEW_EVENT_EXISTING_STORYLINE for a distinct stage/action, NEW_STORYLINE for an identified unmatched occurrence. Shared organization names do not prove identity.
4. Reserve DEFER for genuinely unresolved identity/meaning, not a supporting fact or uncertain future outcome. Keep all quantities, conditions, attribution, negation and time roles.
5. Distinguish graph knowledge from whether this feed published a fact. Group ownership does not automatically make context mandatory.

Minimal relation fields carry the same semantic guidance as descriptions. No new output fields, mechanical gate or inference stage was added. The evidence-free fallback prior is omitted from the wire; actual prior model judgments remain available. Source and mention identities/text are unchanged. `semantic-preparation.ts` includes `articleUnderstandingPolicy=article-occurrence-identity-v1` in the durable construction question, so old paid successes cannot masquerade as evaluations of the changed prompt. Existing UNKNOWN construction fences still apply despite prompt changes. One-repair maximum and all accounting/CAS protections remain.
