This branch adds public Telegram username verification through the private source-execution service, domain and category RSS discovery, provider-free input normalization, richer retained RSS payload metadata, recent Telegram edit rechecks, and an owner-only connector health endpoint. Tests here are local/synthetic. The October 9 live QA results remain recorded separately in `SOURCES_QA_LIVE_2026_10_09.md`.

Deployment must update the VPS `source-execution.py`, `source-execution.mjs`, and server together with the QA Worker. The new `telegram_resolve` operation is unavailable on an older helper. Deploy the additive connector migrations before enabling polling; coordinate shared staging with the intake owner. No provider credits are required for these code-only tests.

Open validation and interface work:

- Prove Telethon session expiry, reconnect/restart recovery, renamed/unavailable channels, recent edit rechecks, and explicit deletion evidence live. Missing messages remain insufficient proof of deletion. The public Telegram page remains partial coverage.
- Resolve X profiles to stable account IDs at approval with a paid-operation fence, and retain reply/quote/thread context only when verified by the provider. Tweet IDs are already stable across TwitterAPI.io and Apify; author IDs are now preferred when available.
- Google News now probes at most eight opaque Google article links per fetch, each with one attempt and a 3-second deadline, without following redirects or fetching publisher pages. Safe HTTPS redirect targets become article URLs; failed, non-redirecting or unsafe targets retain the original Google listing URL. Synthetic tests cover resolution, private-address rejection, failures, probe bounds, request telemetry and stable Google item identity. Live provider behavior still needs a bounded QA check. Cross-source article dedup and EvidenceRevision acceptance belong to intake/downstream. If a later poll first resolves a previously unresolved link but the supplied text is unchanged, the existing intake content-hash replay does not revise published evidence metadata; coordinate any metadata-only revision rule with the intake owner.
- The native RSS provider now optionally fetches and extracts at most two same-origin HTTPS article pages per snapshot when the private source-execution helper is configured. Each article HTTP request gets one attempt and a 3-second deadline; private extraction gets a 5-second deadline. Successful extraction is handed off as `FULL_ARTICLE` with `UNKNOWN` completeness, and the original feed excerpt remains in the payload. Cross-origin articles, failed/challenge extraction and feeds without the helper retain `ARTICLE_EXCERPT`. Synthetic connector and Worker tests cover the bounds and payload. Wider publisher-domain acquisition, article-page redirects, live extraction quality and promoting all preserved metadata to published evidence remain open; the latter needs intake-owner coordination.
- Verify LinkedIn incremental recall, Apify recovery, Playwright escalation, Zyte blocked-site fallback, and uncertain paid outcomes with bounded live tests. Do not infer historical completeness from a successful feed or actor run.
- Extend the health view with per-source paid-operation attribution and retained long-term cost/latency trends. The current endpoint reports durable connector jobs, cursors, batch counts, coverage and recorded failures, without exposing raw provider data.
- Local integration tests now confirm disable/re-enable, individual source removal, tombstoned old intake scope, and enrollment of a newly approved source sharing the same catalog identity. Published support retention remains an intake/publication decision to verify jointly. Shared collection across multiple feeds needs an explicit scope/authorization design; current collection is FeedSource-scoped.
