## Interview and observation guide

Before: Describe a recent monitoring session. What do you need to catch? What takes time? How do you check claims and handle multiple languages?

After each task: What did you decide was important? What evidence supports it? What uncertainty remains? Usefulness 1–5 (higher better); effort 1–5 (higher harder). What did you need to open or verify? What did you think you missed?

After both: Which workflow would you choose and for what tasks? Describe a misleading, omitted or duplicated item. Did language change trust or comprehension? What would prevent regular use? What is the most useful change?

Observe without coaching: start/end, interruptions, source opens, navigation difficulty, answer evidence, unverified assumptions, requests for help, failures and exact participant comments. Distinguish observations from researcher interpretations. Code interviews for monitoring needs, verification, omissions, novelty, multilingual issues, trust, effort and desired changes; retain contrary examples and disagreements between coders.
