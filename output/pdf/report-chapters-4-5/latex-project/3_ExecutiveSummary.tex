\chapter*{Executive Summary}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Executive Summary}

Following the news often requires checking multiple websites, feeds, and social platforms, where reports frequently repeat the same information. The Reuters Institute's \textit{Digital News Report 2026} found that 54\% of surveyed audiences across its markets used social and video networks for news, while 42\% reported avoiding news at least sometimes \cite{reuters2026}. This motivates our project, \textbf{Distilled.News}: helping readers follow the developments that matter to them with less effort and clear access to supporting sources.

The problem addressed is how to turn overlapping reports from different sources into useful, personalized updates. Readers need to distinguish new developments from previously reported information and assess whether a summary accurately represents its sources. The project therefore aims to reduce repetitive reading while preserving the evidence needed to check factual claims.

Our proposed solution is a personalized news platform in which users select their topics, preferred language, sources, and update frequency. The system collects source material, identifies duplicate reports, and selects relevant information while considering what the feed has already published. An AI editor prepares a concise briefing with citations, and a separate AI verifier checks the draft against the collected evidence. When problems are detected, the system attempts targeted corrections before publication. Published briefings are available through public feed pages and optional browser notifications.

The main project constraints are factual accuracy, source permissions, operating costs, processing time, and limited development resources. The system must also support multilingual presentation and recover from interrupted tasks without losing results or publishing duplicates. These constraints guide the use of bounded AI processing, retained source evidence, and checks before publication. However, AI verification remains imperfect and requires independent evaluation.

As of 31 October 2026, we have implemented the main workflow on Cloudflare infrastructure with a React web interface, including source collection, scheduling, storage, briefing generation, verification, publication, and notifications. In one isolated test, the system processed 20 acquired articles and published six briefing items in 41.11 seconds, using four AI model calls costing approximately \$0.0724. The saved results were also successfully retrieved after reopening the database. Separate testing confirmed source transfer integrity in 35 out of 35 checks, but also identified factual and timing errors that passed AI verification \cite{v3twenty,v3overnight}. These results demonstrate technical feasibility while highlighting the remaining work: independent factual assessment, broader multilingual evaluation, realistic failure testing, security and accessibility reviews, and participant studies to measure reader benefit \cite{performanceprotocol,continuity}.
