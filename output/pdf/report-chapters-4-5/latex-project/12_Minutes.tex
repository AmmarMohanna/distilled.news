\section{Weekly Review Record from Repository Evidence}
Our dated development records provide a verifiable record of work carried out each week. We use them below to document technical decisions and follow-up actions. A development record is not a substitute for a meeting record: it does not establish who attended a meeting, what was said, or which decisions were formally approved. We do not assign attendance or invent minutes for meetings whose notes have not been supplied.

\section{Formal Meeting-Minutes Record}
The AUB FYP template includes an official meeting-minutes form. That form is preserved in the editable submission package, in the \texttt{Minutes} folder. For each actual supervisor or team meeting, the completed record should give the date, time, attendees, agenda, decisions, action owners, deadlines, and approval or confirmation where required. Such facts cannot be established from the repository alone. No unsigned retrospective meeting record is presented as an attended meeting.
