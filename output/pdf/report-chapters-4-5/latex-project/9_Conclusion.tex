\chapter{Conclusion}\label{chap:conclusion}
We have demonstrated a functioning, source-preserving personalized briefing pipeline and have progressed beyond a conceptual prototype. Current evidence includes connected source integration, durable storage and publishing controls, native V3 editing/verification/repair, isolated real-source replay and read-back, multilingual frontend/mail/PWA development, preserved integrated implementation, selective database reads, clock-aligned delivery, bounded source recovery and systematic prompt/model comparisons. Importantly, our work also recorded failures: mistaken dates, unsupported news implications, material omitted items and a repair-indexing defect. We corrected mechanical bugs and rejected unproven prompt changes, while keeping open semantic risks visible.

Our central claim at this stage is \emph{engineering feasibility with measured, bounded partial validation}, not complete correctness, sustained service-level compliance, or proven user benefit. A professionally defensible release requires independent truth labels, chronological event/relevance evaluation, runtime and financial observation tied to independently generated opportunities, failure testing, accessibility review, and a controlled user pilot. The schedule and deliverables that follow allocate these remaining tasks by responsibility and decision gate. The project will succeed academically and technically only when the final claim is matched to evidence rather than when the system merely produces polished summaries.
